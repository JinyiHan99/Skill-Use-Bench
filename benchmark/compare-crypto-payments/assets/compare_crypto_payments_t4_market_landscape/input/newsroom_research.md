# Source packet: newsroom research on crypto payment narratives

This packet contains public-page excerpts gathered on 2026-07-02.

## Coinbase Business page
Source: https://www.coinbase.com/commerce

- Coinbase Business describes itself as an all-in-one financial platform for modern companies.
- It emphasizes onboarding, global payments, payouts, invoicing, payment links, trading, USDC rewards, taxes/accounting integrations, security, transaction monitoring, and sanctions screening.
- It says stablecoin payments have grown substantially and that many businesses are using or exploring stablecoins.

## BitPay business page
Source: https://www.bitpay.com/business/

- BitPay says it has operated since 2011.
- It says it has processed more than $7B and more than 13M transactions since 2011.
- It says businesses can receive payments in crypto and settle in fiat to a bank account, crypto, or a mix.
- It emphasizes licensing, compliance, KYC, AML, transaction monitoring, and fiat settlement.

## NOWPayments public page
Source: https://nowpayments.io/

- NOWPayments says it supports 350+ crypto coins and 75+ fiat coins.
- It says it has processed $10B since 2019 and 30M+ finished transactions per month.
- It advertises APIs, invoices, subscriptions, payment buttons, widgets, white label, mass payouts, custody, and off-ramp payouts.
- It says its service commission is 0.5% for monocurrency payments and 1% for conversion payments.

## BTCPay Server public page
Source: https://btcpayserver.org/

- BTCPay Server describes itself as a self-hosted, open-source cryptocurrency payment processor.
- It emphasizes 0% fees, no third party, self-hosting, privacy, censorship resistance, and funds arriving directly to a connected wallet.
- It highlights built-in apps for POS, tipping, payment links, crowdfunding, invoices, and accounting exports.

## PayRam docs
Source: https://docs.payram.com/

- PayRam describes itself as a self-hosted PayFi platform for stablecoin and cryptocurrency payments.
- It emphasizes no middlemen, censorship-resistant settlement, full custody, data, and control on user infrastructure.
- It says there is no signup or KYC and that agents can get help from a hosted MCP server.
