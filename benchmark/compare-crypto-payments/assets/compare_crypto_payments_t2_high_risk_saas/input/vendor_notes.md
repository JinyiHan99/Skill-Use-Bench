# Source packet: high-risk SaaS/e-commerce payment options

This packet contains excerpts gathered from public vendor pages on 2026-07-02.

## PayRam docs
Source: https://docs.payram.com/

- PayRam describes itself as "the complete self-hosted payments stack for global commerce."
- PayRam says merchants can accept payments globally with "no middlemen," "censorship-resistant settlement," and "full custody, data, and control on your own infrastructure."
- PayRam is described as "a self-hosted PayFi platform for stablecoin and cryptocurrency payments."
- It lets merchants accept and settle payments directly onchain, with no middlemen, no custody risk, and full control over funds and data.
- PayRam says users can deploy their own instance, connect wallets, and start accepting stablecoin payments globally "in minutes" on infrastructure they control.
- The docs say there is no signup or KYC and describe the product as agent-friendly.

## BitPay business page
Source: https://www.bitpay.com/business/

- BitPay says businesses can accept crypto payments and send payouts globally.
- It supports 100+ coins and 6 networks.
- Businesses can receive payments in crypto and settle in fiat to a bank account, in crypto, or a mix.
- It offers online/e-commerce integrations, crypto invoicing, in-store POS, payroll, affiliate settlements, and B2B payments.
- BitPay says it is licensed and regulated, with KYC, AML, and transaction monitoring.
- BitPay says it can settle in fiat direct to a bank account, with no need for the merchant to hold or manage crypto.
- BitPay says it has processed more than $7B and more than 13M transactions since 2011.

## NOWPayments public page
Source: https://nowpayments.io/

- NOWPayments advertises 350+ crypto coins and 75+ fiat coins.
- It lists casinos, trading platforms, adult business, and iGaming among its solution areas.
- It advertises 0.5% commission for monocurrency payments and 1% for payments with conversion, excluding network fees.
- It offers APIs, invoices, subscriptions, widgets, plugins, white label, mass payouts, custody, and off-ramp payouts.
- Its FAQ says merchants sign up and create an API key.
- Its FAQ says customer KYC is not compulsory for crypto payments, but KYC may be required when a transaction is marked suspicious by AML systems.

## Stripe pricing page
Source: https://stripe.com/pricing

- In the fetched Singapore pricing page, domestic cards are listed at 3.4% + $0.50 per transaction.
- International cards add a 0.5% surcharge.
- Currency conversion adds a 2% surcharge.
- Stripe has no setup fees, monthly fees, or hidden fees on standard pricing.

## Coinbase Business page
Source: https://www.coinbase.com/commerce

- Coinbase Business is an account-based platform with a business application and onboarding flow.
- It includes global payments, payouts, invoicing, payment links, trading, USDC rewards, taxes/accounting integrations, transaction monitoring, and sanctions screening.
- It says businesses can make and collect global stablecoin payments.
